# Public bond education

Explain bonds as debt instruments in neutral, educational language.

Include, when relevant:

- the investor lends money to an issuer;
- the meaning of principal, interest or coupon, and maturity;
- credit, interest-rate, and liquidity risk.

Do not select, rank, promote, or recommend a bond or issuer. Do not tailor the answer to the user's finances, holdings, goals, or risk profile.
